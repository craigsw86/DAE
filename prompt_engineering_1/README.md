# Prompt Engineering 1 - Course README

## Course Overview
This course introduces students to advanced prompt engineering techniques and AI interaction methodologies. Students learn to design effective prompts, manage AI hallucinations, work with multimodal AI capabilities, and optimize AI interactions across various domains. The course emphasizes practical application of prompt engineering in real-world scenarios.

## Learning Objectives
- Master tokenization concepts and data utilization strategies
- Learn to identify and manage AI hallucinations effectively
- Develop skills in multimodal AI task execution across different domains
- Understand prompt design principles and iterative improvement
- Implement text and image generation workflows
- Create comprehensive documentation of AI interactions

## Course Rubric Requirements

### 1. Tokenization and Data Utilization
- The documentation must include interactions containing at least 3,000 tokens to demonstrate understanding and effective use of tokenization concepts
- Demonstrate comprehensive understanding of token limits and optimization
- Show evidence of effective token utilization across interactions
- Document token counting and management strategies
- Provide examples of token-efficient prompt design
- Show evidence of understanding tokenization impact on AI responses

### 2. AI Hallucination Management
- The documentation must include at least one instance where an AI-generated hallucination was identified and corrected, if present
- Demonstrate ability to identify AI hallucinations and false information
- Show evidence of correction strategies and validation techniques
- Document hallucination detection methodologies
- Provide examples of fact-checking and verification processes
- Show evidence of improving AI response accuracy

### 3. Multimodal AI Task Execution
- The documentation must include AI completing tasks across at least 2 different domains from: programming and scripting, data analysis, text composition, mathematical reasoning, language translation, or creative ideation
- Demonstrate AI capabilities across multiple domains
- Show evidence of domain-specific prompt engineering
- Document task execution strategies for different domains
- Provide examples of cross-domain AI applications
- Show evidence of effective multimodal AI utilization

### 4. Prompt Design and Iteration
- The documentation must showcase at least 3 distinct prompts demonstrating both guided and unguided prompt generation
- Include the AI's responses and documented adjustments or variations made during the prompt testing process
- Demonstrate iterative prompt improvement techniques
- Show evidence of A/B testing different prompt approaches
- Document prompt optimization strategies
- Provide examples of prompt refinement and enhancement

### 5. Text and Image Generation Workflow
- The documentation must include at least 2 instances of text generation and 2 instances of image generation by the AI
- Demonstrate AI's interpretation and response to text prompts
- Show evidence of image generation capabilities and quality
- Document text generation workflows and optimization
- Provide examples of image generation techniques and refinement
- Show evidence of multimodal content creation

### 6. Exported Interaction Documentation
- Submit an export that includes all your AI interactions and outputs
- Ensure that each requirement is clearly demonstrated
- This export can include multiple files or formats as needed to fully showcase your work
- Provide comprehensive documentation of all AI interactions
- Show evidence of systematic interaction tracking
- Demonstrate complete workflow documentation
