# Design 1 - Course README

## Course Overview
This course introduces students to fundamental design thinking principles and user-centered design methodologies. Students learn to conduct user research, create empathy maps, define purpose statements, brainstorm solutions, and develop conversational prototypes. The course emphasizes human-centered design approaches and iterative design processes.

## Learning Objectives
- Master empathy mapping techniques for user understanding
- Learn to conduct and document user and market research
- Develop skills in purpose statement definition and articulation
- Understand brainstorming methodologies and ideation techniques
- Create conversational prototypes for design validation
- Apply design thinking principles to real-world problems

## Course Rubric Requirements

### 1. Create an Empathy Map
- A completed Empathy Map that represents users specific experience
- Demonstrate understanding of user needs, thoughts, feelings, and behaviors
- Show evidence of user research and observation
- Document user pain points and motivations
- Include user goals and challenges
- Provide visual representation of user experience insights

### 2. Document Research
- Documented research (User or Market) about a specific experience or product from at least three (3) different sources
- Demonstrate comprehensive research methodology
- Show evidence of diverse research sources and perspectives
- Document research findings and insights
- Include primary and secondary research methods
- Provide analysis and synthesis of research data

### 3. Define a Purpose Statement
- A documented specific purpose statement that is no more than 2 sentences and includes:
  - What is being made
  - Why it is being created
  - Who will be using it
- Demonstrate clear articulation of design purpose
- Show evidence of user-centered design thinking
- Document the problem being solved and solution approach
- Include target user identification and needs
- Provide clear scope and objectives

### 4. Brainstorm Ideas
- Three (3) documented brainstormed ideas
- Demonstrate creative ideation and divergent thinking
- Show evidence of multiple solution approaches
- Document brainstorming process and methodology
- Include idea evaluation and selection criteria
- Provide visual or written documentation of ideas

### 5. Create a Conversational Prototype
- A conversational prototype that includes, at minimum:
  - A Purpose Statement
  - Research Conducted
  - A visual representation of the product and how it will meet the purpose statement
- Demonstrate prototype development and testing
- Show evidence of user feedback integration
- Document prototype iterations and improvements
- Include visual mockups or wireframes
- Provide clear connection between research and solution

## Application to HIPAA Checklist Project

### Empathy Mapping for Healthcare Professionals
- **User Personas**: Healthcare compliance officers, IT administrators, clinical staff
- **Pain Points**: Complex compliance requirements, time-consuming manual processes
- **Motivations**: Ensuring patient data protection, avoiding regulatory penalties
- **Behaviors**: Regular compliance checking, documentation management
- **Goals**: Streamlined compliance management, automated reporting
- **Challenges**: Keeping up with changing regulations, training staff

### Healthcare Compliance Research
- **User Research**: Interviews with healthcare compliance professionals
- **Market Research**: Analysis of existing HIPAA compliance tools and solutions
- **Regulatory Research**: Study of HIPAA regulations and compliance requirements
- **Competitive Analysis**: Evaluation of current compliance management systems
- **Industry Reports**: Healthcare compliance trends and best practices
- **Stakeholder Interviews**: Input from IT, legal, and clinical teams

### Purpose Statement for HIPAA Project
- **What**: A comprehensive HIPAA compliance checklist and management system
- **Why**: To streamline healthcare compliance processes and reduce regulatory risk
- **Who**: Healthcare organizations, compliance officers, IT administrators, and clinical staff
- **Problem**: Manual compliance tracking is time-consuming and error-prone
- **Solution**: Automated, user-friendly compliance management platform

### Brainstormed Solutions
- **Solution 1**: Interactive web-based compliance checklist with real-time updates
- **Solution 2**: Mobile application for on-the-go compliance checking
- **Solution 3**: Integrated dashboard with automated reporting and analytics
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Usability, compliance accuracy, implementation complexity
- **Selection Rationale**: Web-based solution for maximum accessibility and functionality

### Conversational Prototype Development
- **User Journey Mapping**: Complete compliance workflow from login to reporting
- **Interactive Mockups**: Clickable prototypes demonstrating key features
- **User Testing**: Feedback from healthcare professionals and compliance experts
- **Iterative Refinement**: Multiple prototype versions based on user feedback
- **Validation**: Confirmation that solution meets identified user needs

## Design Thinking Process
```markdown
# Design Thinking Workflow
1. Empathize
   - User interviews and observation
   - Empathy mapping
   - Pain point identification

2. Define
   - Research synthesis
   - Problem definition
   - Purpose statement creation

3. Ideate
   - Brainstorming sessions
   - Solution generation
   - Idea evaluation

4. Prototype
   - Low-fidelity mockups
   - Interactive prototypes
   - User testing preparation

5. Test
   - User feedback collection
   - Iteration and refinement
   - Validation and improvement
```
