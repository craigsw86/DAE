# Risk Management 1 - Course README

## Course Overview
This course provides comprehensive training in risk management fundamentals, assessment techniques, evaluation methods, mitigation planning, risk communication, and implementation strategies. Students learn to develop, implement, and manage enterprise risk management programs using industry-standard frameworks and methodologies. The course emphasizes practical application to real-world organizational scenarios.

## Learning Objectives
- Master risk management fundamentals and regulatory frameworks
- Learn risk assessment techniques and evaluation methods
- Develop skills in risk mitigation planning and control selection
- Understand risk communication and stakeholder management
- Implement comprehensive risk management programs
- Apply risk management frameworks to organizational scenarios

## Course Rubric Requirements

### 1. Risk Management Fundamentals
- Create a project risk framework document using NIST RMF or ISO 31000 standards with proper risk terminology (threat, vulnerability, asset, control) applied to a realistic scenario
- Develop governance structure documentation showing roles, responsibilities, and decision-making authority with escalation procedures
- Include regulatory compliance matrix for one industry (GDPR, HIPAA, SOX, or PCI-DSS) and visual framework mapping showing organizational alignment
- Documentation must be professional quality with clear formatting suitable for executive presentation
- Show evidence of comprehensive risk management framework implementation

### 2. Risk Assessment Techniques
- Conduct structured risk identification using three methods (brainstorming, checklists, SWOT analysis) with documented facilitation approach
- Apply qualitative analysis using 5x5 risk matrix with likelihood and impact scales, plus quantitative analysis including EMV and ALE calculations with supporting data
- Complete threat and vulnerability assessment with documented findings
- Create comprehensive risk register with scoring rationale and historical tracking capabilities
- Show evidence of comprehensive risk assessment methodologies

### 3. Risk Evaluation Methods
- Develop multi-criteria risk scoring model using weighted evaluation factors (financial, operational, compliance, reputation) with defined measurement scales
- Perform Business Impact Analysis including RTO, RPO, and MTD calculations with financial impact analysis and dependency mapping
- Document evaluation criteria with stakeholder approval
- Create critical asset risk exposure register showing threat exposure, vulnerabilities, and interconnection dependencies with professional formatting
- Show evidence of comprehensive risk evaluation capabilities

### 4. Risk Mitigation Planning
- Select and document controls using industry frameworks (NIST Cybersecurity Framework, ISO 27001) with detailed rationale and cost-benefit analysis
- Document risk treatment options for all four strategies (mitigate, avoid, transfer, accept) with implementation timelines and resource requirements
- Develop comprehensive mitigation strategy aligned with business objectives
- Calculate residual risk after control implementation with effectiveness measurements and monitoring procedures
- Show evidence of comprehensive risk mitigation planning

### 5. Risk Communication
- Create structured executive risk report with key findings, financial impact analysis, and actionable recommendations formatted for board presentation
- Develop stakeholder communication plan with audience analysis, tailored messaging, and communication schedules
- Design visual risk dashboard with key metrics, automated alerting, and drill-down capabilities
- Implement KPI framework with leading/lagging indicators and tracking systems with documented review processes
- Show evidence of comprehensive risk communication capabilities

### 6. Risk Management Implementation
- Develop complete risk management program plan with realistic implementation timelines, resource allocation, and phase-based deployment strategy
- Implement chosen framework (NIST RMF, ISO 31000, COSO) with documented customization, gap analysis, and integration procedures
- Create comprehensive risk register with historical tracking
- Establish continuous monitoring system with automated indicators, threshold alerting, and environmental change detection capabilities for ongoing program management
- Show evidence of comprehensive risk management implementation

## Risk Identification Methods

2. **Checklists**: Healthcare risk assessment checklists
3. **SWOT Analysis**: Healthcare strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats

## Qualitative Risk Analysis
- **5x5 Risk Matrix**: Likelihood (1-5) x Impact (1-5)
- **Risk Scoring**: Risk score = Likelihood × Impact
- **Risk Categories**: Low (1-4), Medium (5-12), High (15-25)

## Quantitative Risk Analysis
- **EMV Calculation**: Expected Monetary Value = Probability × Impact
- **ALE Calculation**: Annual Loss Expectancy = SLE × ARO
- **Cost-Benefit Analysis**: Control cost vs. risk reduction benefit

## Healthcare Risk Evaluation
```markdown
# Healthcare Risk Evaluation Framework

## Multi-Criteria Risk Scoring
- **Financial Impact**: 40% weight (revenue loss, fines, legal costs)
- **Operational Impact**: 30% weight (system downtime, service disruption)
- **Compliance Impact**: 20% weight (regulatory violations, audit findings)
- **Reputation Impact**: 10% weight (brand damage, customer trust)

## Business Impact Analysis
- **RTO (Recovery Time Objective)**: 4 hours for critical systems
- **RPO (Recovery Point Objective)**: 1 hour for patient data
- **MTD (Maximum Tolerable Downtime)**: 8 hours for clinical systems
- **Financial Impact**: $1M per hour of downtime

## Critical Asset Risk Exposure
- **Patient Data Systems**: High exposure to data breach threats
- **Clinical Systems**: High exposure to ransomware threats
- **Medical Devices**: Medium exposure to IoT security threats
- **Network Infrastructure**: High exposure to network attacks
```

## Healthcare Risk Mitigation
```markdown
# Healthcare Risk Mitigation Strategy

## Control Selection Framework
- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework**: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover
- **ISO 27001**: Information security management system controls
- **HIPAA Security Rule**: Administrative, physical, technical safeguards

## Risk Treatment Options
1. **Mitigate**: Implement security controls to reduce risk
2. **Avoid**: Eliminate risk by not performing risky activities
3. **Transfer**: Transfer risk through insurance or outsourcing
4. **Accept**: Accept risk when cost of mitigation exceeds benefit

## Healthcare Risk Controls
- **Administrative Controls**: Policies, procedures, training
- **Physical Controls**: Facility security, device protection
- **Technical Controls**: Encryption, access controls, monitoring
- **Operational Controls**: Incident response, business continuity

## Residual Risk Calculation
- **Inherent Risk**: Risk before controls (e.g., 20)
- **Control Effectiveness**: Control effectiveness percentage (e.g., 80%)
- **Residual Risk**: Inherent Risk × (1 - Control Effectiveness) = 4
```
