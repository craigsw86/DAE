# Cyber Threats and Vulnerabilities 2 – Project Rubric

This rubric outlines the advanced requirements for the project in the areas of threat analysis, vulnerability management, threat intelligence, risk management, and platform development. Each criterion must be met at an advanced level for full credit.

---

## Grading Scale
- **1:** Complete
- **0:** Incomplete

---

## Criteria

### 1. Advanced Threat Analysis
- Demonstrate advanced APT campaign analysis, including malware reverse engineering with detailed technical documentation.
- Analyze complex attack patterns with demonstrated zero-day research methodology and findings.
- Complete a comprehensive supply chain vulnerability assessment with clear documentation of methods and results.
- All analysis must include:
  - Detailed methodology documentation
  - Technical findings
  - Indicators of compromise
  - Mitigation recommendations
- **Evidence:** Reverse engineering outputs, attack pattern analysis, vulnerability assessment results.
